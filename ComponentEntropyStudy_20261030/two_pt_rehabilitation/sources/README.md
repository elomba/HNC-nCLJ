# Primary method sources

- T. Sun, J. Xian, H. Zhang, Z. Zhang and Y. Zhang, *Two-phase thermodynamic model for computing entropies of liquids reanalyzed*, J. Chem. Phys. **147**, 194505 (2017). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5001798 ; author manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02041 . Spectrum normalization, classical weights, signed residual and analytical Lorentzian tail, gas/solid partition and normalized diffusivity (Appendix A), and same-density vs same-pressure HS reference (Appendix B).
- P.-K. Lai, C.-M. Hsieh and S.-T. Lin, *Rapid determination of entropy and free energy of mixtures from molecular dynamics simulations with the two-phase thermodynamic model*, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. **14**, 15206–15213 (2012). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/C2CP42011B . Mixture partial-volume prescription and the one-fluid equal-partial-volume approximation. The paper's mixing-excess quantities are not conflated with ideal-gas excess entropy in this test.

Downloaded PDFs and rendered pages are only a local literature cache and are ignored by git. No published numerical fit was recalibrated to the present TI results.
